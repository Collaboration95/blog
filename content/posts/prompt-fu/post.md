Built Prompt-Fu for AI GrandPrix  , a 24hr hackathon , co-hosted by OpenAi & Coupang at Carousell Sg. 

The first individual challenge had a simple brief. Build a game that's fun, creative and polished, and publish it on Sites.

I hadn't used ChatGPT Sites for a project with a backend before. The Site opens the game in an iframe, while a separate Node server serves the game and connects the phones over WebSockets.

I built Prompt Fu, a couch party game inspired by Boomerang Fu. One screen runs the arena. Scan a QR code and your phone becomes the controller.

Describe a snack and Codex turns it into a fighter, complete with a weapon. Describe a place and it becomes the arena theme. Then throw boomerangs, dash, hide in bushes and try to be the last snack standing.
