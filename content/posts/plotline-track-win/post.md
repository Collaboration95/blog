Last week I took part in Hack & Hire: Sustainability Edition, a week-long hackathon at NUS, and won our track 🏆

In case you missed my last post: 

Our solution speeds up the EU's deforestation check for farm plots and makes the data it needs much lighter. 

The stored maps are 99% smaller, checks are about 5× faster, and re-checks are up to 44× faster.
