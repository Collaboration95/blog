Meetings are great at producing decisions. Less great at getting them into Slack and CRMs.

At the AI Tinkerers Agents, Everywhere hackathon, our team built Handoff: a finance workflow agent that lives inside a LiveKit meeting.

Ask it by voice to prepare an invoice, and Handoff checks the signed contract, pricing, fulfilment, quantities, taxes, payment terms, and source evidence. If anything is missing or conflicts, it stops for human review. Before creating anything, the exact invoice plan is shown for approval; only then does it create and verify an Airwallex sandbox draft.

The goal was simple: let teams discuss, approve, and complete a financial action in the same place ( with an audit trail, not blind automation)
Built with a great team in a very intense day. 🚀

We built it with AgentKit, GPT-Live-1, Langfuse, Airwallex MCP for developers and some good old Python.
