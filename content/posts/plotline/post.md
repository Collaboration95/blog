If you sell products like rubber, coffee or wood in the EU, you have to prove they didn't come from deforestation. It involves checking every farm plot against years of satellite maps and doing it again whenever they update. 

Last week I built Plotline at Hack&Hire: Sustainability Edition, for SystemEarth geospatial challenge to help 

Our project makes that check must faster and lighter. ( 99% smaller , 5x faster on average , 44x faster on rechecks from the starting point )  

check it out -> https://lnkd.in/gweFY29x
Every dot in the scroll animations is a real map cell, and the dots regroup into each step of the pipeline as you scroll (best on a computer).
