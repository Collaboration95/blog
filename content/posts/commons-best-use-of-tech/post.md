Yesterday I won Best Use of Tech at the Common Grounds make-athon, and won a 1000$ in codex credits. 

It just goes to show you cant prepare for everything. I had stayed up until 2:30am the night before, and by morning agents had prototyped ten ideas for me. 

At 10:01 the organisers went through the event slides. At 10.04 , I had dropped every idea that i prepped for. 

I built The Commons, a room that builds itself. Everyone in the room scans a QR code and wishes for a creature. Codex checks the wish, writes it as a small program, tests it, fixes its own bugs and draws it.

 About a minute later it walks into a cartoon of the room you're sitting in, with your name on it.I ran the day like a product team of one. 

A spec by late morning, two builds in parallel, and a demo video cut minutes before submission.

I entered as a solo dev , but it doesnt mean i could have done it alone.
