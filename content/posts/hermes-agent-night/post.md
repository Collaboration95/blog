Hermes AI Agent Night at AWS was a blast!

Jiamin Wang And I are getting ready to present our Research Agent at AI Tinkerers ( Saturday Demo Meetup with Joe Heitzeberg ),  a project we built during Singhacks 2026. 

We went along to learn more about deploying our agent on AWS and where Hermes might fit into what we’re building.

Nathaniel Ng gave us a helpful walkthrough of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Ashish Verma demoed 8mem, the memory layer for AI agents. It was useful to see these tools in action and think about how we could apply them to our own project.

There were plenty of other interesting demos throughout the evening, too.

Really enjoyed meeting Shaun-Paul L. and chatting with other people about what they’re building.

Huge Thanks to Lionel Sim and May Yang and the Amazon Web Services (AWS) team for bringing everyone together.
