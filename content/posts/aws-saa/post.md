"Excited to announce" that I recently passed the AWS Certified Solutions Architect (SAA-C03) from Amazon Web Services (AWS) — scored 911/1000!

Three certs down now: Cloud Practitioner, AI Practitioner, and now SAA.

This one felt different. Cloud Practitioner and AI Practitioner were mostly about knowing what AWS services exist. 
SAA-C03 is about knowing why you'd pick one service over another and how they fit together at a system level.

My timeline looked like this:

3 weeks of study —> working through the slides and labs in
Stéphane Maarek's SAA-C03 course on Udemy.

1 week of review —> practice tests on Udemy, plus the section-based and topic-based tests on TutorialsDojo.

The area that pushed me the most was networking VPCs, subnets, and routing, all of it. It took a second pass through the slides and working through some tricky scenarios with Claude before it really clicked.

Once it did, a lot of the architecture questions started making a lot more sense.

If you're thinking about taking the SAA-C03, I'd strongly recommend starting with Stéphane Maarek's course on Udemy. The way he builds up from first principles and ties each service to real use cases made the difference for me, especially the focus on architecture-related questions covered during the course.

Pair it with TutorialsDojo for practice tests, and you'll be in good shape.
