# contact-sheet
**Idea.** Every post is a strip of film on a darkroom contact sheet: frames sit whole on black, and a grease pencil marks them and carries across the gutter onto the words.
**System.** 36 posts are 36 strips, numbered newest first. Each image is a lettered frame (`35C`). Text-only posts are text frames at a chosen exposure (`frame 7 exp 1`). Marks come only from `marks.md`. From 1024px a post with frames is two strips side by side: text left, frames right, a gutter between them. Each frame floats from its own sentence, which then runs on unbroken, and clears the frame above it. A post with no frames is one strip, centred.
## Type, colour, grid
- Archivo (variable, 125 KB) is the only family. Body is 18/29 at weight 430 on a phone and 20/32 on a wide screen, about 65 characters to the line. Edge codes and dates are condensed caps.
- Paper `#060605`, text base `#141312`, rebate `#1b1916`, text `#dcd7cb`, dim `#a79f90`, red `#ff4a30`, yellow `#ffd338`, date stamp `#ff8c2e`.
- Dark earns its place by being two darks: frames sit on true black so photos glow, text sits on a lifted base at 12.9:1 instead of 14.7:1, a touch heavier, so a 37-block post doesn't halate.
- Phone: rail 26px, sprocket pitch 20px, pad 15px. Wide: rail 31, pitch 24, pad 38, text 600 at most, gutter 4vw, side strip 640 at most.
- Phone index: frames after the lead wrap into justified rows, about two across, median 167px.
## Details I like
1. Sprocket rails are SVGs generated by `build.mjs`, so their proportions are defined once.
2. Pencil strokes pass through an SVG turbulence filter and look waxy.
3. On a wide screen a lead leaves the mark, crosses rails and gutter, then runs along the gap between two lines to reach its word without striking the words before it.
4. Approximate dates print as `c 10 '26` on the orange stamp.
5. In `apple-health-import` the side strip holds exposed words. The next paragraph waits for each one, so reading order survives, and the pencil chain 26 minutes, 21 minutes, 9.77 seconds crosses the gutter twice.

## Authoring
```
![04](once the organisers explained more. ^ Picking the new idea)
![02 in](that redrew ^ the venue photo.)
(o red)[2 hours 47 minutes]
@04 ring 69 26 9 5.5 -> 2 hours 47 minutes
```
`^` splits the sentence and frame 04 lands there: in line on a phone, level with that line on the side strip on a wide screen. `in` keeps a frame in the text strip on a wide screen, which breaks the column and takes up the slack when frames run longer than text. `side 3` hangs a lede at the top of the side strip. A post with no entry gets the automatic rule in `build.mjs`.

**Transfers:** whole images at real ratio, lettered frame numbers, the approximate-date form, floats that clear each other. **Stays here:** rails, dark paper, pencil leads across text.
**Weakest:** at 1440 a text-only post is still one strip between black flanks, and a post with one or two frames leaves a long run of blank frames on the side strip. With JS off a frame has no full-size link (`check.mjs` forbids links out of the concept), and the first frame stays at its sentence instead of rising to the top.
